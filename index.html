About this app

How to operate the site

  1. Add players — tap Add Player to type or paste names in, or Check In to bring in players who pre-registered.
  2. Watch the queue — the Players panel shows everyone waiting, grouped into the next matchups in order.
  3. Start a game — once a court has enough players lined up, tap the court to start the match.
  4. End a game — tap the court again, enter the score (optional), pick the winner, and confirm to send everyone back to the queue.
  5. Winners / Losers blocks — when that mode is on, winners and losers are held separately and queued back up once a full group is ready — use Queue now to release a group early.
  6. Rankings & history — check the 🏆 and 🕓 icons up top for standings and a full match log.

Hosting live

  1. Sign in — tap the ☁️ cloud icon and log in or create a free account (needed to host; watching never requires one).
  2. Go live — tap Go live to broadcast your current courts and queue. You'll get a short invite code, a shareable link, and a QR code — share any of the three.
  3. Spectator view — anyone who opens the link or scans the QR sees a live, read‑only view of the courts, "Up next," and the accumulating winners/losers blocks, refreshing every few seconds. If scoring is on, they see the current score too — as a read‑only number, not tappable controls. No account or app needed on their end.
  4. Notify me — spectators can tap the bell in their banner to get a browser notification when a game ends or the next matchup changes.
  5. Daily limit — each account can go live a limited number of times per day (shown in the panel); the count resets daily.
  6. Stop / resume — tap Stop hosting to end the broadcast at any time; you can resume on the exact same code and link afterward, or start fresh.
  7. Watch someone else — got a code from another host? Paste it into the same ☁️ panel's "Match code" field and tap Watch to follow their session live.

What's in Settings

  1. Session name — the title shown at the top of the app and on the share/spectator link.
  2. Game format — Doubles (4 players) or Singles (2 players) per court, applied across all courts at once.
  3. Matching style — choose Balanced, Skill Separated, or Winners/Losers to control how the queue forms matches.
  4. Number of courts — add or remove courts, and set each court's name and display skill level.
  5. Sound on call‑up — plays a chime when a court is ready to start.
  6. Target Games — auto-tracks games played per player and prioritizes whoever has played the fewest, until everyone hits the target you set.
  7. Avoid Repeating Teammates — tries not to pair the same two players together twice in a row; you can also lock a Fixed duo that always plays together.
  8. Enable Scoring — adds a live scoreboard (serve tracking, point steppers, side‑outs) to each in‑progress court, feeding straight into End Game.
  9. Auto‑start Ready Courts — if a court has enough players and nobody taps Start within 2 minutes, it starts itself. Turn off to always start manually.
  10. Manage known players — rename saved players, change their player type, or remove a saved name used for quick‑add suggestions; changes here don't affect anyone currently checked in except a rename, which updates them everywhere they currently appear.
  11. Backup — export or import the full session as a JSON file.
  12. Session controls — end the session (keeps all records), start a new session (keeps player names), or erase everything.

Player buttons on a court

  1. InfoInfo — tap to see a player's full name, skill level, Fixed Duo pairing, and this session's games/wins at a glance.
  2. SwapSwap — tap one player's Swap button, then tap another's, to trade their court spots (fixes a wrong pairing or just remixes the teams). Works before or during a match. Tap the same button again to cancel. Disabled for players locked into a Fixed duo.
  3. SubSubstitute — tap a player's Sub button to bring in a replacement from the queue when someone has to step away. The game continues on the same court with the same score/timer, and the player subbed out goes to the back of the queue.
